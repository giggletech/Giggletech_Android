APP NAME
GiggleTech

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Connect VRChat to GiggleTech haptics over Wi-Fi, directly from your phone.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Bring your VRChat interactions to life with GiggleTech for Android.

Use your phone to route OSC from VRChat on Quest or PC to your GiggleTech Wi-Fi haptic devices. Keep your phone, VRChat device and hardware on the same network, add your device settings, and start routing.

YOUR HAPTICS, YOUR SETTINGS
Find GiggleTech hardware on your Wi-Fi and select a device to fill in its address. Add multiple devices with individual names, avatar parameters and power limits. Choose proximity-based output or velocity-sensitive haptics, with smoothing and pull-away controls.

CONNECT WITH OSCQUERY
Find VRChat on your network and advertise the router through OSCQuery. Manual UDP port configuration is also available.

STAY IN CONTROL
View incoming OSC activity and motor output levels, test each device with a short low-power pulse before connecting to VRChat, and stop routing from the app or its ongoing notification. Routing can continue with the screen off.

LOCAL BY DESIGN
No account, advertising, analytics or cloud service. Configuration stays on your phone, and communication happens between your configured network devices.

REQUIRES COMPATIBLE HARDWARE
GiggleTech Wi-Fi haptic hardware, an Android 8.0+ phone, and an OSC-enabled sender such as VRChat are required. This app does not use the phone's vibration motor. Network isolation, firewalls and battery-management settings may affect connectivity.

Stop routing before disconnecting the network or powering off the phone. Hardware without a firmware watchdog may retain its last command if communication is interrupted.

GiggleTech is not affiliated with VRChat or Meta.
